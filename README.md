## What I'm building toward

The gap I keep running into: banks and fintechs have the models. They don't have the infrastructure to get those models into production in a way that survives a regulatory audit, a change freeze, and a 3am incident simultaneously. That gap — between AI capability and AI deployment in regulated environments — is what I'm focused on closing.

__Long-term__: I want to work at the intersection of **credit risk infrastructure**, **agentic AI deployment**, and **healthcare data systems** — the three sectors where the cost of getting AI wrong is high enough that human-in-the-loop engineering will always matter.
